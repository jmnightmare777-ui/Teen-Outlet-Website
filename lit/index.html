WHAT IS LIT?

Leadership starts with character.

LIT — Leaders in Training — is a developing Teen Outlet opportunity designed to help students grow in faith, responsibility, teamwork, and leadership.

This is our starting point. As LIT develops, we can add program details, applications, meeting times, expectations, team roles, mentors, photos, and resources.

THE HEART OF LIT

Grow from the inside out.

01

Faith

Grow through prayer, Scripture, worship, and discipleship.

02

Character

Develop consistency, humility, responsibility, and integrity.

03

Leadership

Learn to influence others well and lead by example.

04

Service

Find practical ways to serve Teen Outlet, the church, and community.

05

Teamwork

Learn to support a team, solve problems, and follow through.

06

Purpose

Discover strengths, gifts, and opportunities to make a difference.
