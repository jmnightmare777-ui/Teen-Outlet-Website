WHAT IS LIT?

Leadership starts on the inside.

Before leadership is a title, it is faithfulness, humility, responsibility, and the willingness to serve.

LIT — Leaders in Training — is being built to help students develop those foundations through discipleship, mentorship, teamwork, practical responsibility, and opportunities to lead by example.

THE LIT PATHWAY

Rooted. Equipped. Trusted. Released.

The program can expand as details are finalized, while this page remains the home for applications, schedules, expectations, training resources, and team updates.

ROOTED

Faith first

Grow through Scripture, prayer, worship, discipleship, and a personal walk with God.

EQUIPPED

Skills that serve

Practice communication, teamwork, preparation, problem-solving, and follow-through.

TRUSTED

Responsibility

Build consistency, integrity, humility, and the confidence to handle real assignments.

RELEASED

Lead by example

Use your gifts to serve Teen Outlet, the church, the community, and the people around you.

THE HEART OF LIT

Growing leaders who make a difference.

01

Faith

Keep Jesus at the center of every decision, responsibility, and opportunity.

02

Character

Choose integrity, humility, consistency, and responsibility when nobody is watching.

03

Leadership

Influence people well, communicate clearly, and lead through example instead of position.

04

Service

Notice needs, take initiative, and use your strengths to help someone else move forward.

05

Teamwork

Support the team, receive feedback, solve problems, and follow through on commitments.

06

Purpose

Discover your gifts and learn how God can use them in ministry and everyday life.
