TESTIMONY

God changes everything.

About a year ago, I started coming to Teen Outlet with absolutely no expectations. I had no idea how much God was going to change my life through this ministry.

Over the past year, I’ve had the privilege of watching so many young people come to Christ, grow in their faith, and completely change their lives.

Now, doors have opened for me to serve as a leader in this ministry, something I never would have imagined when I first walked through those doors.

God has given me a confidence I didn’t have before, and I’ve seen such a difference in my anxiety and the way I face things.

Teen Outlet has shown me that sometimes God puts you somewhere without you knowing why, and then you get to look back and see exactly what He was doing.

I’m so thankful for everything God has done in me and through me over this past year, and I can’t wait to see what He does next.

— Emma
