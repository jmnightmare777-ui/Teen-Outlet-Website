MISSION · VISION · ROADMAP

Building more than a youth ministry.

Our goal is to meet young people where they are, help remove the barriers in front of them, and build a pathway that leads toward faith, stability, purpose, leadership, and a future they can step into with confidence.

OUR MISSION

Reach. Equip. Launch.

Teen Outlet exists to reach young people where they are, surround them with support, help them grow in faith and purpose, and connect them with the relationships, resources, and opportunities they need to move forward in life.

OUR VISION

A complete support system.

We envision Teen Outlet becoming a comprehensive ministry campus where young people can find discipleship, education support, transportation, job preparation, mentoring, housing support, leadership development, and practical pathways toward stability and independence.

OUR GOAL

Reproduce the solution.

We do not want to help young people for only a moment. We want to reach them, equip them, launch them, and develop disciples and leaders who return to help reproduce solutions for the next generation.

WHERE WE ARE GOING

The Teen Outlet roadmap.

The vision grows in stages. Each partnership today helps build the foundation for what can serve young people tomorrow.

01 · NOW

Build the foundation

Strengthen the ministry and relationships already reaching young people today.

  • Weekly ministry and discipleship
  • Community outreach and events
  • LiT leadership development
  • Launchpad support
  • Business and community partnerships
02 · NEXT

Expand practical support

Remove the barriers that keep young people from moving forward.

  • Transportation and ministry vehicles
  • Education recovery and GED pathways
  • Job readiness and career training
  • Mentoring and life skills
  • Stronger student-support programs
03 · FUTURE

Build the campus

Create a permanent home where multiple programs can work together under one mission.

  • Dedicated Teen Outlet facility
  • Administration and ministry offices
  • Classrooms and training spaces
  • Transportation resources
  • Housing and transitional support pathways
  • Employer and education partnerships
04 · BIG VISION

Launch and multiply

Develop young people who leave stronger and return ready to strengthen others.

  • Faith and discipleship
  • Education and employment
  • Stability and independence
  • Leadership and service
  • Disciples who make disciples
  • Young adults who become solution builders
Reach them → support them → develop them → launch them → send them back to help others.

The long-term vision is a place where a young person can arrive with limited resources or direction and leave with faith, education, employment, stability, community, leadership, purpose, and the ability to help someone else find the same hope.
