THE NEXT STEP IN THE MISSION

Practical support with lasting purpose.

Teen Outlet Launchpad Ministry is being developed to help young adults overcome real-life barriers through transportation, employment, housing assistance, life-skills development, mentorship, and support systems.

The goal is not simply to meet immediate needs. It is to help individuals move toward stability and independence, build a sustainable future, and eventually help others do the same.

AREAS OF SUPPORT

From barriers to pathways.

01

Transportation

Helping create reliable access to work, school, appointments, and opportunity.

02

Employment

Job-readiness support, mentorship, and connections that encourage stability and growth.

03

Housing assistance

Support and guidance aimed at helping young adults move toward a safe, stable living situation.

04

Life skills

Practical tools for budgeting, planning, communication, responsibility, and independent living.

05

Mentorship

Consistent relationships with people committed to walking alongside young adults.

06

Support systems

Connecting people with resources, community, and next steps that help them keep moving forward.
