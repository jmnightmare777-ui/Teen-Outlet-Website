MEET PASTOR TREY & HALEE

A heart for people & purpose.

TO

Pastor Trey and his wife, Halee, have a heart for seeing young people know Jesus, discover their purpose, and find a place where they truly belong.

In 2021, God placed a vision on Trey’s heart for something different—a ministry that would go beyond a weekly service and become a place where young people could be seen, known, understood, and loved. That vision became Teen Outlet.

Since then, the vision has continued to grow. Teen Outlet is about more than getting young people through the doors of a church. It’s about walking with them through real life, building genuine relationships, helping meet real needs, discipling them in their faith, and showing them what the love of Jesus looks like beyond a Sunday or Wednesday service.

Trey and Halee are raising their two children, Harper and Elijah, and family is at the heart of who they are. That same heart shapes Teen Outlet. Their desire is for every young person who comes through the doors to know that they have a place, they have people in their corner, and they never have to walk through life alone.

Today, Trey is committed to following where God leads and continuing to build the vision God placed on his heart—loving young people, investing in them, pointing them to Jesus, and helping them become everything God has called them to be.

Teen Outlet isn’t about building a crowd. It’s about building people, changing lives, and becoming family along the way.
