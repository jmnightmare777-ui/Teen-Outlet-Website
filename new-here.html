QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

Know before you go.

What should I wear?+

Teen Outlet is casual. Wear what you normally wear and come ready to connect.

Can I come by myself?+

Yes. Leaders and students are there to help new people feel welcome and find their place.

Do I need a church background?+

No. Teen Outlet welcomes young people who are familiar with church and those who are exploring faith for the first time.

What happens during the night?+

The gathering typically includes community, worship, a message, prayer, and time to connect.

How can a parent contact Teen Outlet?+

Call (636) 725-3351, email teenoutlet21@gmail.com, or message the Teen Outlet Facebook page.
